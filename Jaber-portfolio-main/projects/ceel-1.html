Noir Coffee

A promotional poster design for a coffee shop concept.

Project Overview

Noir Coffee is a promotional poster created as part of the CEEL 301 Graphics Design activities. The design focuses on presenting a coffee shop concept through typography, layout, and visual presentation.

Design Concept

The poster uses a warm brown and cream color palette to establish a coffee-inspired visual identity. Typography and organized content help present the promotional information clearly.

Design Elements

  • Promotional poster layout
  • Warm brown and cream color palette
  • Typography and visual hierarchy
  • Menu and price presentation
  • Organized visual composition

What I Learned

This activity helped me understand how color, typography, and layout work together to communicate a promotional message. It also helped develop my understanding of visual hierarchy in graphic design.
